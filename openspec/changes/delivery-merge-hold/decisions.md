# Decisions and ADRs

- ADR: `docs/adr/0025-a-roadmap-run-grants-one-merge-turn-at-a-time.md` — A roadmap run grants one merge turn at a time (amends ADR 0002, ADR 0003 and ADR 0013 decision 4 as ADR 0020 and ADR 0021 extended it, for a project without a delivery train; ADR 0022 governs a project with one)
  - Decision 4: a change leaves by itself on a failed review, a new commit, a return to plan, red CI or a conflict outside the release files (or one that cannot be classified); such a conflict never joins, and a queued change leaves once and then gets the plain conflict stop, so join and leave cannot alternate; an ADR renumber is work of the turn, handed only to the holder, never a reason to leave.
  - Decision 5: the queue exists only where devkit enables auto-merge (the `merge` mode); `trunk` and `two-prs` read no queue.
  - Decision 6: the queue and its turn live on `origin` as refs outside the branches (`refs/devkit/`), created or deleted only by pushes with an expected old value, with a bounded retry; it states why ADR 0020's rejection of a claim ref does not apply to the queue.
  - Decision 7: the turn arrives through the change's own background wait on GitHub (ADR 0011), which reads the turn at each poll; nothing passes the turn by time, by the owner's grill answer; the run's one session cron (ADR 0021 decision 4) may carry the coordinator's `wake` but grants no turn, and a queued change's wait is not one of the stale wait loops it stops; it states why ADR 0020 decision 5's rejection of a background poll of places does not apply to the turn (a change counted from `origin` waiting for a place has no pull request and no wait on GitHub; a change waiting for the turn already keeps one).
  - Decision 9: a project with an accepted `devkit.delivery.train` records no queue: its roadmap run pushes no marker, the change's turn is null, and no change or archive of it gets `join-queue`, `leave-queue` or `wait-turn`, because a boarded change is not a keep step and would join and leave on alternate calls until `no-progress`; ADR 0022 governs that project, this record a Claude-driver run in a `merge`-mode project without a train; a CompozyOS run (`compozy-start`, ADR 0024) records no queue; combining the turn and the train needs its own record.
  - Alternatives: the delivery train of ADR 0022 as the only answer (opt-in, `trunk` and `trunk-auto` only), the turn applied in a train project too (withholds nothing, alternates), a timer or the run's periodic check passing the turn (set aside by the owner's answer; the check fires about every 30 minutes, the wait sees a merge at its next poll), a file on a branch of `origin` (ADR 0020's choice of the branch, set aside for the queue) and the coordinator's message alone (ADR 0020's choice over a poll, set aside for the turn).
