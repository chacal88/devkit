- [ ] roadmap-sessions-upfront (after: roadmap-coordinator-followups) — asked by the owner on 2026-11-22: when `/devkit roadmap <n>` starts, it opens every change it will run, each in a session of its own, at once; at most `<n>` changes hold a place for the stages that load the machine (implementation, gates, evidence, review), and the others wait in their sessions under a waiting stage icon until a place frees. ## Round 28: remote worker
- [ ] remote-worker (after: roadmap-coordinator) — a change can run on another machine: the project configures a command that starts a change there and one that asks its state, and the roadmap coordinator chooses where each change runs. Required by the owner on 2026-11-21: no machine opens a change that is already open on another one.
